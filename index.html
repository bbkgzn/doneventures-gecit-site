04 / Founder

Babak Gözen

Founder · Pharmacist, MSc · Entrepreneur

Babak Gözen is a pharmacist and entrepreneur with an MSc in Pharmaceutical Technology and experience in pharmaceutical product management, international business and independent pharmacy operations.

His background spans healthcare, product development and business initiatives, bringing together practical industry knowledge and an interest in emerging technologies, creative ventures and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Through Done Ventures, he explores new ideas, develops initiatives and connects with people who share an entrepreneurial mindset.
